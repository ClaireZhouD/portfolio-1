About the Artist

Claire Zhou is an independent fine art photographer based between Stockholm and Berlin. Her practice centers on intimacy, transience, memory, and the unspoken poetry found within domestic everyday environments.

Working predominantly with 35mm film, medium format cameras, and available natural light, her images capture delicate gestures, shadow plays, and quiet human presence without post-processed manipulation.
